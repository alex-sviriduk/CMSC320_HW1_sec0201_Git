Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git provides a structured framework to record the entire evolution of a machine learning or analytical project.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be applied to the healthcare field to improve patient diagnoses and treatment plans.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging preserves the original history with a new commit, while Rebasing rewrites history to create a linear timeline.
